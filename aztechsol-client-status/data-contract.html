Every outbound project email should be generated from the same parent/child Notion structure so the workflow stays reliable.

This page is the system contract behind the email templates.

Rule:
If a value needs to appear in a client email or internal payout email, it should come from this structure — not from ad hoc notes or hand-entered email copy.
