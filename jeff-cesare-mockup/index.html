Site Strategy

What the new site should actually do

Jeff’s current site establishes legitimacy, but it does not yet separate his personal credibility from his available opportunities. The new direction should make him feel organized, selective, and easier to trust.

1

Build trust fast

Sharper branding, clearer positioning, stronger testimonials, and a more commercial presentation style.

2

Show opportunities clearly

A dedicated Properties section with reusable listing templates, highlight-sheet style visuals, and a polished teaser format.

3

Filter weak leads

Keep details intentionally limited, steer traffic into a contact flow, and collect enough context to cut down on tire-kickers.

Template ingredients

What each listing page should include

  • hero image + property title
  • quick facts and high-level highlights
  • location and market context
  • map / aerial placeholders or visuals
  • strong call to contact Jeff directly
  • minimal teaser copy instead of exhaustive detail
