# Image Prompts — The Wings of a King

All prompts use the same style suffix. Generate in portrait or wide landscape as noted.

**Style suffix (append to all):**
> Epic fantasy digital painting, classical oil painting style, warm golden amber color palette, rich sepia tones, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, atmospheric haze and mist, painterly brushwork, highly detailed, cinematic composition, in the style of Greg Rutkowski and Donato Giancola, romantic and contemplative mood, no cool tones, warm firelight atmosphere.

---

## 1. COVER — "The Wings of a King" (already generated ✅)
The king with his damaged iron machine, bird people in the amber sky, scattered white feathers.

## 2. "Among the Bird People" (wide landscape)
A young man in a white uniform with great white feathered wings, soaring above golden clouds alongside other winged people. Below them, a patchwork of tiny kingdoms and oceans. The sky is endless amber. He looks free, weightless, joyful. The wings are bright and full.

## 3. "The Child Across the Ocean" (portrait)
A winged man in a white uniform cradling a small infant against his chest mid-flight over a golden ocean. The baby's tiny hands grip his shirt. Behind them, the silhouette of Paris — the Eiffel Tower barely visible through warm atmospheric haze. Other winged figures fly alongside in loose formation. Tender, protective, intimate.

## 4. "The Nest" (wide landscape)
Two birds — one still building, one looking away — perched on an elaborate nest woven from golden twigs, set high on a stone balcony overlooking a vast kingdom. The nest is beautiful but fraying on one side. Some twigs have been pulled loose. Warm sunset light, melancholic but not bitter.

## 5. "The Forge" (portrait)
A man standing at a blacksmith's forge, his white wings folded behind him but visibly fading — translucent, ghostly, dissolving into particles of light. He grips a hammer. The forge glows hot. He is choosing to become something new. The wings are becoming embers. The transformation is happening.

## 6. "The Plague and the Silent Sky" (wide landscape)
An empty amber sky over a medieval city. In the foreground, a man works alone at a desk inside a castle window, lit by warm candlelight. Outside, the sky that once held hundreds of flying bird people is completely still. A few white feathers drift down like snow. Eerie, quiet, isolated but safe.

## 7. "The Dwarven Jet" (portrait)
A dark iron motorcycle — but reimagined as a fantasy war machine, forged by dwarves. Glowing runes along the engine block, exhaust pipes like dragon breath, leather and iron and fire. It sits in a stone workshop, freshly forged, gleaming. The king stands beside it, no longer winged, wearing leather armor and a crimson cloak, reaching out to touch it for the first time. Awe and hunger in his posture.

## 8. "Flight Without Feathers" (wide landscape)
The king riding the iron machine through a vast Arizona-like desert at golden hour. His crimson cloak streams behind him. The road stretches to the horizon. Dust and golden light. No wings — but the same feeling. Speed, wind, freedom. The clouds are close. He is almost flying.

## 9. "The Fall" (portrait)
The iron machine lies on its side on a desert road, scratched and dented. The king walks away from it, looking back over his shoulder. He is not broken — but he is shaken. Dust settles. The sunset is blood-orange. Scars on the machine mirror scars on the man.

## 10. "The Mending" (portrait)
The iron machine sits in a dwarven forge, partially disassembled, being repaired. Sparks fly. The king stands in the doorway watching, arms crossed, crimson cloak over one shoulder. He hasn't decided to ride yet. But he's here. That's the first step. Warm forge light, hopeful.

## 11. CLOSING — "The Castle at Dawn" (wide landscape)
A castle at first light. The king stands on a balcony, looking east. In the courtyard below, the repaired iron machine waits. In the sky above, faint traces of bird people flying — memories, not ghosts. Two small figures (princes) play in the courtyard near the machine. The kingdom is quiet, warm, alive. A new day. Everything is possible.
