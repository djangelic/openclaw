✈️ Flight Information

⏰ Timezone Math: Barcelona is CET (UTC+1). If you're coming from the US, factor in the time difference when booking flights. US West Coast travelers (MST/PST) are 8-9 hours behind — you need to fly out Saturday Mar 7 to arrive Sunday Mar 8.

🛫 Barcelona–El Prat Airport (BCN)

  • Airport code: BCN (Josep Tarradellas Barcelona–El Prat)
  • Distance to city center: ~18 km (~30 min by taxi/Aerobus)
  • Getting to city: Aerobus (€7.75, every 5 min to Plaça Catalunya), Metro L9 Sud, taxi (~€40), or Uber/Cabify
  • Terminal: Most international flights arrive at Terminal 1

🗺️ Barcelona — What to Know

🚕 Getting Around

  • Metro: Excellent system, covers most tourist areas. T-Casual card (10 rides for ~€11.35)
  • Walking: City center is very walkable — most sights within 30 min walk of Plaça Catalunya
  • Taxis: Yellow/black, metered, reliable. Also Uber and Cabify
  • Bus Turístic: Hop-on hop-off tourist bus if you want a quick overview

📱 Useful Apps

  • TMB App — Barcelona metro/bus planner
  • Cabify / Uber — Ride hailing
  • Google Translate — Camera mode for menus/signs
  • Google Maps — Download offline map of Barcelona before you go

⚡ Quick Tips

  • Power: EU Type C/F plugs — bring an adapter if coming from US/UK/Israel
  • Tipping: Not expected in Spain, but rounding up or 5-10% is appreciated for good service
  • Meal times: Lunch is typically 1:30–3:30 PM, dinner 8:30–10:30 PM (later than most countries!)
  • Pickpockets: Be aware on Las Ramblas and in the Metro — keep phones/wallets secure
  • SIM/eSIM: EU roaming is free for EU residents. US visitors: check with carrier or get an eSIM (Airalo, Holafly)
  • Water: Tap water is safe but many prefer bottled

🚶 Neighborhoods to Explore

  • Gothic Quarter (Barri Gòtic): Medieval streets, Barcelona Cathedral, hidden squares. Best for wandering.
  • El Born: Trendy bars, boutiques, Picasso Museum, Basilica de Santa Maria del Mar
  • Las Ramblas: Iconic boulevard from Plaça Catalunya to the port. Touristy but a must-walk.
  • Barceloneta: Beach neighborhood — great seafood, waterfront walks
  • Gràcia: Local bohemian vibe, less touristy, great for dinner
  • Eixample: Grid-plan neighborhood with most Gaudí buildings, great restaurants

🍽️ Food & Drink

  • La Boqueria Market (Las Ramblas) — famous food market, great for a quick bite or fresh juice
  • Tapas: Bar del Pla (El Born), Cervecería Catalana (Eixample), Cal Pep (seafood tapas near El Born)
  • Paella: Try it near Barceloneta beach — Can Paixano (La Xampanyeria) for cheap cava + tapas
  • Pintxos: Basque-style tapas bars in El Born area
  • Brunch: Federal Café, Flax & Kale (healthy options)
  • Cocktails: Paradiso (hidden speakeasy, world-famous), Dry Martini, Bobby Gin
  • Wine: Spain is incredible for wine — order local Catalan wines (Penedès, Priorat)
🍷 Team Dinner Ideas:
• Can Culleretes — One of Barcelona's oldest restaurants (est. 1786), traditional Catalan
• 7 Portes — Classic seafood/paella, good for groups
• El Nacional — Beautiful multi-space food hall in Passeig de Gràcia (great for groups with different tastes)
• Tickets — Creative tapas by the Adrià brothers (book WAY in advance)

🏟️ Other Activities

  • Camp Nou (FC Barcelona): Stadium tour + museum (~€28) — new renovated stadium opening 2025-2026!
  • Montjuïc: Castle, gardens, Fundació Joan Miró, Magic Fountain (evening light show)
  • Picasso Museum: In El Born — book online to skip the line
  • Cable car: Port cable car for aerial harbor views
  • Day trip: Montserrat monastery (~1hr by train) — spectacular mountain views
