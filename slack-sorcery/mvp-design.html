🧠 Design Philosophy: Psychology First

Every mechanic is grounded in proven game psychology. We're not guessing — we're applying frameworks from Bartle's Player Types, Nir Eyal's Hook Model, Skinner's Variable Ratio Reinforcement, and Self-Determination Theory. The game should feel addictive before it feels complex.

🎰 Variable Ratio Reinforcement

B.F. Skinner — most addictive reward schedule

Random encounters at unpredictable times. Random Pokémon species. Random catch rates. Like slot machines — the uncertainty is the hook.

Random spawns Catch rates Loot drops

📈 Endowed Progress Effect

Nunes & Drèze — people complete goals they've started

Start with a starter Pokémon immediately. You're not at zero — you're already 1/151. The Pokédex progress bar does the rest.

Starter pick Pokédex % Collection

😰 Loss Aversion

Kahneman & Tversky — losses hurt 2× more than gains

Lose a Pokémon in PvP? That stings. You'll fight harder to get it back. Stakes make every battle matter.

PvP wagers Steal mechanics Emotional stakes

🏆 Bartle's Player Types

Richard Bartle — 4 player motivations

  • Achievers: Pokédex completion, XP, levels
  • Explorers: Rare spawns, hidden encounters
  • Socializers: Trading, team battles, chat
  • Killers: PvP, leaderboards, stealing

🪝 The Hook Model

Nir Eyal — Trigger → Action → Variable Reward → Investment

  1. Trigger: Slack notification ("A wild Pikachu appeared!")
  2. Action: Tap one button to battle
  3. Variable Reward: Did you catch it? What moves does it know?
  4. Investment: Train it, level it, add to your team

🎯 Self-Determination Theory

Deci & Ryan — intrinsic motivation

  • Autonomy: Choose your team, your strategy
  • Competence: Get better at type matchups, timing
  • Relatedness: Trade, battle, collaborate with coworkers

⚖️ Encounter Economy & Pacing

Spawn Schedule (Configurable)

Frequency

  • Casual: 2-3 spawns/day (friends server)
  • Active: 4-6 spawns/day (engaged team)
  • Intense: 8-12 spawns/day (gaming server)

Admins set in App Home → Settings

Rarity Distribution

  • ⬜ Common: 50% (Pidgey, Rattata, etc.)
  • 🟦 Uncommon: 30% (Eevee, Machop, etc.)
  • 🟪 Rare: 15% (Dragonair, Alakazam, etc.)
  • 🟨 Legendary: 5% (Mewtwo, Mew, etc.)

Anti-Monopoly Rules

  • Cooldown after catch: can't claim next encounter (30 min)
  • Max active team: 6 Pokémon (forces strategy)
  • Duplicate Pokémon: keep max 2 (trade or release extras for coins)
  • Legendary limit: 1 of each (if you lose it in PvP, it's gone until you recatch)

⚔️ Battle System — Simple but Strategic

Battles auto-resolve using Pokémon stats from PokeAPI. Players don't choose moves in wild encounters (keeps it fast). In PvP, they pick one move per round via modal.

Why Auto-Battle for Wild Encounters?

Reducing Friction — The psychology is clear: every additional step between trigger and reward reduces engagement. Wild encounters should be one tap. The fun is in the result (did I catch it?), not the process. Save interactive battles for PvP where the stakes and social element justify the friction.

Think poker: You're dealt a hand (your team), the flop is the wild Pokémon. You decide to play (battle) or fold (pass). The cards play themselves. Your edge comes from team building and type knowledge, not clicking buttons.

🚀 MVP Build Order

v0.1   The Hook (1-2 weeks)

Goal: People open Slack and see a Pokémon. They tap a button. Something fun happens. That's it.

  1. Onboarding modal — /slackemon → pick starter from Gen 1 starters (Bulbasaur, Charmander, Squirtle)
  2. Random spawn — Schedule trigger → PokeAPI → Block Kit encounter message
  3. One-tap battle — Button click → auto-battle → catch roll → result posted
  4. App Home profile — Team, collection count, XP bar
  5. D&D Node storage — Player + Pokémon state

✅ Covers: Hook Model (trigger→action→reward→investment), Variable Reinforcement, Endowed Progress

v0.2   The Stakes (1-2 weeks)

  1. PvP challenges — /challenge @user → team select modal → interactive battle
  2. Wager system — winner takes a Pokémon
  3. Leaderboard — App Home tab, weekly auto-post to channel
  4. GIF effects — Battle animations via Giphy/Tenor API

✅ Covers: Loss Aversion, Social Proof, Competence/Mastery

v0.3   The Economy (1-2 weeks)

  1. Trading — DM-based trade proposals with confirmation modals
  2. Shop — Spend coins on Poké Balls (better catch rate), potions (heal team)
  3. Pokédex view — Full grid with caught/uncaught silhouettes
  4. Achievements — Auto-react milestones, special badges on profile

✅ Covers: Reciprocity, Collection Completion (Zeigarnik), Autonomy

v0.4   Themes & Polish

  1. Theme engine — Admin settings modal to switch Pokémon / DnD / Pirates / Space
  2. AI narration — Flavor text for encounters and battles
  3. Custom emoji support — Upload Pokémon as workspace emoji for avatars
  4. Workplace rewards integration — Hook for external reward APIs
