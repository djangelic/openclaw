🎮 Blended Mechanics — The Best of Three Worlds

We're pulling from D&D, Magic: The Gathering, and tactical RPGs. Here's what comes from where:

D&D Stats & Progression

  • 6 core stats: STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA
  • Stat checks happen automatically (you don't roll — the bot does)
  • Level up after X encounters → pick new abilities
  • Classes define your starting card deck
  • HP, AC (armor), and basic attack/defend

MTG Card-Based Abilities

  • Each player has a hand of 5 cards
  • Cards = abilities (spells, attacks, buffs)
  • Play 1 card per round + basic attack
  • Mana system: regenerates each round
  • Loot = new cards for your deck
  • Deck building between encounters

Tactics Grid & Positioning

  • 5×5 emoji grid in Slack messages
  • Move 1-2 tiles per turn
  • Flanking bonus (adjacent ally = +damage)
  • Terrain matters (🪨 cover, 🔥 hazard)
  • Range: melee (adjacent) vs ranged (2+ tiles)
  • AoE spells hit grid patterns

How Movement Works

  • In the Action Modal, pick a direction: ⬆️⬇️⬅️➡️ or stay
  • Heavy armor = 1 tile, light/none = 2 tiles
  • Flanking: if 2+ allies adjacent to enemy → +50% damage
  • Cover: behind 🪨 = ranged attacks have disadvantage
  • Hazards: standing on 🔥 = 2 damage/round
  • All movement resolves simultaneously (collisions = both stop)

Think Final Fantasy Tactics or Into the Breach — simple grid, big decisions.

Example Cards by Class

⚔️ Fighter

  • Shield Bash (1 mana) — Push enemy 1 tile + stun
  • Cleave (2 mana) — Hit all adjacent enemies
  • Rally (1 mana) — Adjacent allies +2 ATK this round

🧙 Mage

  • Fireball (3 mana) — 3×3 AoE, 8 damage
  • Frost Wall (2 mana) — Create 🧊 row, blocks movement
  • Arcane Sight (1 mana) — Reveal enemy next action

🏹 Ranger

  • Snipe (2 mana) — Any tile, ignores cover
  • Trap (1 mana) — Place on tile, triggers on step
  • Shadow Step (1 mana) — Teleport 3 tiles

Card Rarity & Loot

Like MTG, cards have rarity that affects power:

  • ⬜ Common — Basic abilities, low mana
  • 🟦 Uncommon — Stronger effects, combos
  • 🟪 Rare — Powerful, deck-defining
  • 🟨 Legendary — 1 per deck limit, game-changers

Combo System:

If two players play cards with matching tags in the same round, they combo:

  • 🔥+🔥 = Inferno (double fire damage)
  • 🧊+⚡ = Shatter (frozen enemies take 3× lightning)
  • 🛡️+🛡️ = Phalanx (all allies +4 AC this round)

Combos reward coordination without requiring voice chat!

🎯 What a Player Actually Experiences

Scenario: Tuesday Morning in #tavern

  1. 📱 9:00 AM — You get a Slack notification: "A wounded orc staggers into the tavern, axe raised!"
  2. 🗺️ The message shows a 5×5 grid. The orc is at position C2. Your fighter is at E4. Your friend's mage is at A3.
  3. ⚡ You tap "Take Action" button → modal opens
  4. 🎮 In the modal you see:
    • Move: dropdown → you pick "Up 2 tiles" (now at E2, adjacent to orc)
    • Card: your hand shows 5 cards → you pick "Shield Bash" (1 mana, push + stun)
    • Target: 👹 Orc
  5. ✅ You submit. Bot says "Action locked in! Waiting on 1 more player..."
  6. 🧙 Your friend (at lunch) opens their modal, picks Fireball targeting tile C2
  7. 🎲 Both actions resolve simultaneously:
    • You moved to E2 and Shield Bashed for 6 damage + stun
    • Friend's Fireball hits C2... but you pushed the orc to C1! Fireball hits empty tile 😅
    • BUT — your Shield (🛡️) + their Fire (🔥) triggered no combo this time
  8. 📖 AI narrates: "The fighter charges forward, slamming his shield into the orc's jaw. The beast staggers backward just as a column of fire erupts where it stood a moment ago. The mage curses from across the room. The orc, dazed but alive, snarls..."
  9. 🗺️ Updated grid posted. Round 2 begins. You have 4 hours to submit.

📈 Progression & Meta-Game

Short Term (Per Encounter)

  • Survive the fight
  • Earn XP + gold
  • Draw loot cards (new abilities!)
  • Spend gold at the shop modal between fights

Medium Term (Per Week)

  • Level up → unlock class upgrade tree
  • Build/refine your card deck
  • Unlock harder encounter tiers
  • Complete quest chains (AI-driven story arcs)

Long Term (Per Campaign)

  • Boss encounters (multi-round, complex grids)
  • Party reputation score (affects shop prices, NPC interactions)
  • Leaderboards in a pinned channel message
  • Seasonal campaigns (vector store loads different story)

Social Hooks

  • 🏆 Achievement reactions (auto-react milestones)
  • 📊 Weekly stats post ("Angel dealt 847 damage this week")
  • 🃏 Card trading between players
  • ⚔️ PvP arena (optional 1v1 channel)
